National arrears bottomed in 2007-06 (GFC window) and 2022-08 (recent window). From trough to roughly 18 months later (2008-12 vs 2024-02), the national arrears rate rose by 8.6 basis points after the GFC trough and by 4.4 basis points after the recent trough, so the current cycle looks slower over that horizon. Between the two episodes the reporting panel changed: Manulife joined in 2004, Laurentian in October 2010 (after the GFC trough window’s early months), and Equitable in November 2020, so the recent cycle includes banks that were not in the GFC-era panel.
